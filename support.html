关于本 App

「东游记·八仙过海」是一款水墨国风的神话视觉小说（文字冒险）游戏，改编自中国民间「八仙过海」传说。游戏分两条主线：第一章讲述八仙各自历经成仙之劫，第二章随八仙横渡东海、各显神通，内含回合制战斗与选择分支。

  • 五语言支持：简体中文、繁体中文、英语、日语、韩语；
  • 水墨手绘背景、角色立绘与原创配乐；
  • 完全离线、无广告、无内购、无登录。

系统要求

iPhone 设备，iOS 15.0 或更高版本，支持 arm64 架构。在 iPad 上以兼容模式运行。

常见问题

如何开始新游戏？

在主菜单点击「新游戏 · 入门」即可从第一章开始。点击「继续 · 再历」可回到上次保存的进度。

如何切换语言？

主菜单底部有五个语言按钮，点击即可即时切换，设置会自动保存。

游戏进度会丢失吗？

进度仅保存在本机。若卸载 App 或清除其数据，进度将被删除；正常更新 App 不会影响已保存的进度。

遇到闪退或画面异常怎么办？

请先将系统升级到最新的 iOS 版本，并尝试完全关闭后重新打开 App。若问题仍然存在，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，并附上设备型号与系统版本。

战斗怎么玩？

回合制战斗中可使用三个技能攻击敌人，并留意血条与怒气。合理选择技能即可击败心魔与摩揭。

About the App

"Eastern Record: The Eight Immortals Cross the Sea" is an ink-wash, Chinese-mythology visual novel (text adventure) adapted from the Chinese folk legend of the Eight Immortals. Chapter One follows each immortal through their personal trial of ascension; Chapter Two joins them as they cross the Eastern Sea, each revealing their own divine power, with turn-based battles and branching choices.

  • Five languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean;
  • Hand-painted ink-wash backgrounds, character portraits, and original music;
  • Fully offline, with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no login.

System Requirements

iPhone running iOS 15.0 or later on arm64 architecture. Runs in compatibility mode on iPad.

FAQ

How do I start a new game?

From the main menu, tap "New Game · Enter the Gate" to begin Chapter One. Tap "Continue · Resume the Trial" to return to your saved progress.

How do I switch the language?

There are five language buttons at the bottom of the main menu. Tap one to switch instantly; your choice is saved automatically.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes progress; normal App updates do not affect saved progress.

What if the App crashes or displays incorrectly?

Please update iOS to the latest version and try fully closing and reopening the App. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and iOS version.

How does combat work?

In turn-based battles, use three skills to attack while watching your health and rage. Choose skills wisely to defeat the Inner Demon and Mo Jie.
